## Target and decision

The target requires separation for **every** allowed positive parameter. Two model classes were kept distinct: a homogeneous stationary reversible single-reticulation model with three observable cycle attachments, and a four-cycle with independent positive nonsingular general-Markov channels. Tree comparators and stronger closure/mixture conclusions are specified in the manuscript. Arbitrary networks, unrelated edge-specific GTR generators on triangles and triangle orientation are not claimed.

Decision: **proceed on the specified residual claims with explicit attribution and no first-ever claim**. The commissioned revision permits reproduction, exposition and a distinct extension. The known JC special case is credited in the article; its reparameterisation is given there once. The new rank target was rechecked before its derivation.
